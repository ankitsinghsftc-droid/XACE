## Proposed first-party continuing-use scope — not in force

Subject to the owner's rights in the covered XACE materials, the proposed developer permission is:

- Developers retain ownership of their game-specific content, assets and gameplay work.
- Developers may ship their games with the matching XACE runtime, launcher and game adapters, and continue running those shipped games without Builder, an XACE account or XACE cloud availability.
- Developers may retain, modify and rebuild the XACE-owned runtime/launcher/adapter and game-executor sources supplied in their approved per-game continuity archive for developing, maintaining and redistributing that game.
- Stopping use of Builder does not revoke those game-maintenance/shipping permissions or require deleting the developer's game source, saves or shipped runtime copies.
- These permissions do not authorize reselling the XACE Builder/service or grant ownership of third-party engine code/assets. Required dependency notices and third-party terms remain applicable.
- Approval creates no claim that all game mechanics, platforms, runtime-free export, store integration or external release tests are certified.

The owner must confirm authority and approve or amend that exact scope and the applicable distribution/notice matrix before an active license/terms artifact is issued. Pricing, support obligations, warranties, legal entity/publisher identity and third-party rights are not invented by this task. A general instruction to finish NEC is not recorded as approval of previously unspecified licensing terms. No signed candidate currently exists, so final candidate-specific rights approval also remains open.
